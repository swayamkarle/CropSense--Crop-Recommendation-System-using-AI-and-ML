Soil Types & Characteristics

Alluvial Soil (30.8%)

Rich in minerals, ideal for rice, wheat, sugarcane, maize, cotton, soybean, and jute cultivation.

Black Soil (19.2%)

High clay content, excellent for cotton, wheat, jowar, millets, linseed, castor, and sunflower.

Clay Soil (30.8%)

Dense and heavy, suitable for rice, lettuce, chard, broccoli, cabbage, and snap beans.

Red Soil (19.2%)

Iron-rich, best for cotton, wheat, pulses, millets, oilseeds, and potatoes.

📊 Distribution Insights

The dataset is carefully balanced with Alluvial and Clay soils representing 40% each of the training data, while Black and Red soils each comprise 25%. This distribution ensures the model learns to distinguish between all soil types effectively, leading to the impressive 99% accuracy rate in production.

Key Benefits: Balanced training prevents bias, ensures consistent performance across all soil categories, and provides reliable crop recommendations for diverse agricultural regions.
